# Batch 2.4 — Team and access by department: the contract

**Written:** 4 November 2026, before any code, from the plan
(`2026-10-30-phase-2-frame.md`, *Batch 2.4* and *Choices* 2, 5, 6, all
approved with the recommendation on 30 October), frame 5 of
`docs/design/hba-os-shell-2026-10-25.html`, and `main` at `37fdd94`
(which includes ADR 0073: the content manager invites models). Backend and
frontend are built in parallel against this; a change to it is made here
first.

"The owner" means whoever holds `settings.manage` (the `admin` role). Every
route below is the owner's (`settings.manage`); anybody else gets 403, as
`/api/staff` does today.

## 1. Nothing about how access is stored changes

A **level** is one of today's roles, shown in new words. No table, column or
migration. Giving and taking a level is the existing `give_role` /
`remove_role` (`app/shared/services/staff.py`) with every rule they keep:
nobody changes their own access; nobody grants or takes what they do not
hold (`can_grant`); the last active owner stays; one operations manager at a
time; the last staff role cannot be taken (suspend instead). Each step is
audited as today (`staff.role_given`, `staff.role_removed`).

### The map (one place: `app/core/permissions.py`, `DEPARTMENT_LEVELS`)

| Department | No access | Viewer | Staff | Manager |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `inbox` (Inbox) | — | not offered yet | `customer_service` | not offered yet |
| `operations` (Operations) | — | not offered yet | not offered yet | `operations_manager` |
| `affiliates` (Affiliates) | — | not offered yet | `content_manager` | `affiliate_manager` |

- The order of departments is the sidebar's (Inbox, Operations, Affiliates).
  Production, Finance and People are **not drawn** (Choice 2), though frame 5
  draws Production and Finance disabled.
- **Owner** (`admin`) is not a department level. It is its own line above the
  departments (§3).
- A person's level in a department is the highest level whose role they hold
  there (Manager > Staff > Viewer); "none" when they hold none. Somebody
  holding both Affiliates roles reads as Manager; choosing a level takes the
  other one away (§2).
- **Words** (Choice 6; stored names unchanged): `admin` → **Owner**,
  `operations_manager` → **Operations manager**, `customer_service` →
  **Inbox staff**, `affiliate_manager` → **Affiliates manager**,
  `content_manager` → **Affiliates staff**. Used wherever a role is shown to
  staff: Team and access, invitations, the sidebar's line under a name
  (`roleLabel`), and the activity log's sentences (`audit_words._ROLE`:
  "Gave Mona Inbox staff access"). Nothing that is stored or sent to the
  server changes.

## 2. `PUT /api/staff/{user_id}/access/{department}`

Body `{"level": "none" | "viewer" | "staff" | "manager"}`. Sets that one
department to that level **in one transaction**:

1. the level's role is given if not already held (`give_role`), **then**
2. every other role of that department is removed (`remove_role`).

Giving before taking means a change of level never trips "this is the only
access". `"none"` only removes; when it would leave nothing, the existing
refusal stands ("This is the only access Mona has. Give them other access
first, or suspend the account."). Any refusal rolls back the whole change:
no half-changed department.

- Unknown department → 404. A level not offered there → 422 "Not offered
  yet". Same level as now → 200, nothing written.
- Refusals keep today's status and words (400/403/409 as `give_role` /
  `remove_role` raise them today).
- Answer: the person's row as `GET /api/staff` gives it (§3), after the change.

`POST /{id}/roles` and `/{id}/roles/{role}/remove` stay (the Owner line uses
them for `admin`; nothing else in the app calls them after this batch).

## 3. `GET /api/staff` (same route, more in each row)

Each `staff` row keeps every field it has and gains:

```jsonc
{
  "owner": false,                       // holds admin
  "access": { "inbox": "staff", "operations": "none", "affiliates": "manager" },
  "access_words": "Inbox staff · Affiliates manager",   // frame 5's small line; "Owner" for an owner
  "joined_at": "2026-09-03T…"           // = created_at
}
```

The answer gains, beside `role_permissions`:

```jsonc
"departments": [
  { "key": "inbox", "name": "Inbox",
    "levels": [ { "level": "none", "role": null, "offered": true },
                { "level": "viewer", "role": null, "offered": false },
                { "level": "staff", "role": "customer_service", "offered": true },
                { "level": "manager", "role": null, "offered": false } ] },
  …
],
"role_words": { "admin": "Owner", "customer_service": "Inbox staff", … }
```

Each `invitations` row gains `access_words` ("Inbox staff") and keeps `sent_at`.

## 4. Two-step reminder: `POST /api/staff/{user_id}/two-step/remind`

Frame 5's *Send a reminder* (on a person whose two-step sign-in is off).
Emails them one short message: what two-step sign-in is, that their access
does not require it (or does, if it does), and where to turn it on
(Settings → Me). 201 `{"emailed": true}`; 409 when it is already on; never on
yourself (400). One per person per 24 hours (429 "A reminder was sent at
…"). Audited `staff.two_step_reminded`, read as "Reminded Mona to turn on
two-step sign-in". A new notification kind in the outbox, like the others.

## 5. The page (`/settings/team`, frame 5)

**Left: the people list.** Each active or suspended person: avatar initials,
name, `access_words`, a lock where two-step sign-in is on
(`two_step_enabled`). Then "Invited, not joined yet": each waiting
invitation's address and "Inbox staff · sent yesterday". The head says
"N people · M invitations waiting" with **Invite someone**. Selecting a
person opens their page on the right (address `?person=<id>`, so a reload
keeps it); the first person is opened when none is chosen.

**Right: a person's page.**
- Name; email · joined date.
- **Owner** line: "Owner — every department, Settings and money" with Give /
  Take (confirmed, as today). While somebody is the owner, the department
  rows are shown but disabled, with "The owner reaches every department."
- **A row per department** with the four levels as a segmented control
  (`role="radiogroup"`); levels not offered are drawn disabled with
  "Not offered yet" (tooltip and visually-hidden text). Choosing a level
  asks for confirmation naming what changes (as today's dialog does), then
  calls §2.
- **What this gives {first name}:** one or two sentences built from the
  permissions their levels grant together, with what they do *not* get (as
  frame 5: "No Operations, no pay figures, no payroll or payments…"). Built
  in the frontend from `role_permissions` with one phrase per permission;
  a test proves every permission any role grants has a phrase, and that the
  sentence for each level names exactly what the server grants.
- **Two-step sign-in** (On/Off pill, required or not, *Send a reminder* when
  off, *Reset* for a lost phone as today), **Signed in on** with *Sign out of
  every device* (today's sessions and end-all), **Suspend** (danger box, the
  frame's words) / **Reactivate** for a suspended person.
- For yourself: everything shown, nothing changeable ("You cannot change
  your own access").

**Invite someone** opens a dialog: email, then **Owner**, or a department
and a level (only levels offered, and only roles in `invitable_roles`); it
sends the same `POST /api/auth/invitations` with the level's role. Pending
invitations keep *Resend* and *Withdraw* on their own page (selecting one in
the list).

**390:** frame 5 has no phone drawing. The list is the page; choosing a
person opens their page full width with a back link "Team and access". The
segmented control wraps to two rows of two buttons, each at least 44px high.

## 6. Tests the batch must carry (from *Done when*)

- Backend: each level maps to exactly its role and back; §2 gives and takes
  the right roles, audited, in one transaction, and rolls back on any
  refusal; every existing rule still refuses through §2 (own account,
  `can_grant`, last owner, one operations manager, last role); not-offered
  levels 422; §4's limits; an invitation by level grants the same as by role.
  Non-owners get 403 on every new route.
- Frontend: the list and a person's page from a roster; choosing a level
  calls §2 and shows the result; not-offered levels cannot be chosen; *What
  this gives* covers every permission and matches the server for each level;
  invite by level sends the right role; the role words everywhere.
- **Visual:** frame 5 at 1280; our own 390.
- On staging: the owner gives the test account a level and takes it back;
  the test account's sidebar changes after signing in again.

## 7. Risks

This page decides who can do what, so the independent review is told to
attack the access rules in §2 above all: a level change must never grant
what `give_role` would refuse, never leave a half-changed department, and
never reach the owner's own account.
