What you are approving

  1. Inbox has two sections: Chats and Confirmations. There are no settings, calls, statuses or groups.
  2. Chats is three columns on a laptop: conversations, the chat, the order. On a phone it is one at a time.
  3. Four ways to narrow the list: All, Unread, Wants a change, Cancelled, and a search by name, phone or order number.
  4. The customer's name and phone are shown in the Inbox. They are not shown anywhere in Operations.
  5. Every reply shows who sent it. Old replies brought over from the previous inbox show “HBA”, because the sender was never recorded.
  6. Staff can set an order to confirmed or cancelled from the chat. That changes the tag in Shopify. It does not cancel the order in Shopify; that is still done there.
  7. After 24 hours of silence the typing box is replaced by an explanation and the approved messages. The second message shown here, “We tried to reach you”, does not exist yet and would need Meta's approval.
  8. Old photos, voice notes and files show as no longer available. WhatsApp deleted them and the old inbox kept no copy.
  9. Confirmations lists only cash-on-delivery orders, with a Send again button that refuses an order already messaged.
  10. Operations · WhatsApp shows five cards: Failed to send, No WhatsApp, Never messaged, Answers not recorded, Waiting over 24 hours.
  11. The operations manager gets a switch between Operations and Inbox.
