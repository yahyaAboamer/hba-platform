What you are approving

  1. The operations manager's own shell: Shopify, Bosta and E-stebdal in the same sidebar the admin tool uses, and nothing else. On a phone they become tabs across the top.
  2. Cards that open their list below the cards, one at a time, oldest first, each order linking out to Shopify, Bosta or E-stebdal. Long lists show the first rows and a Show the other N button.
  3. The success rate as one figure with a bar, not Bosta's ring: a single share reads faster as a number. The range chips sit above it.
  4. Picked up per day as seven rows with a bar each, today first; Friday is drawn as a day off rather than a zero.
  5. "Updated N minutes ago" on every tab; amber with a clock when older than 10 minutes; red with a warning when the source can't be read, keeping the last good numbers greyed with their time. Never a 0 for a failure.
  6. The admin's switch under the brand: Affiliates | Operations. The six affiliate sections stay six.
  7. Settings: Operations manager in Team with the one-manager refusal, a new line in the access table, and Bosta and E-stebdal keys beside the Shopify connection.

Where this departs from the approved export: Operations has no export, so its shell, cards, chips and tables are the export's own pieces, reused. The switch and the success-rate bar are new. Both are recorded against ADR 0057 once approved.
