HBA OS: one frame for every department

How Inbox, Operations and Affiliates, and later Production, Finance and People, sit in one sidebar instead of three dashboards with a switch. It is drawn with the product's own colours, type, cards and tables. The screens inside each department do not change; the frame around them does. Every name, phone number, order and amount here is made up, and nothing is built yet. Use View as to see what each kind of person gets.

        New: one sidebar, departments inside it

        What you are approving

        1. One sidebar replaces the three dashboards and the switch. Departments in this order: Today, Inbox, Operations, Affiliates, Production, Finance, People, with Settings at the bottom.
        2. Only the department you are in shows its sections. The others stay folded and show a count only when something in them waits for you.
        3. Each person sees only the departments they hold. Someone who holds one department skips Today and lands in it, as customer service lands on Chats now.
        4. The start page is called Today, not Home, because the affiliate side and the model portal already have screens called Home. The affiliate side's Home becomes Affiliates → Overview.
        5. Today lists what waits for you across departments, broken things first, then the longest waiting, each with one button that opens it where it is done.
        6. Search sits at the top of the sidebar and opens with Ctrl K. It finds customers, orders, models and products, and only what you may see.
        7. Every order has one page, reachable from every department. Each section shows only to holders of its department; the customer's name and phone still show only to Inbox holders.
        8. Department screens keep their names and look. Their addresses move under the department (Affiliates → Payments), old links redirect, and new departments use different words (Salaries, Bills) so nothing appears twice.
        9. Access is set per department, at four levels, and one person can hold several. In Finance, approving and paying stay apart: only the owner does both.
        10. Two-step sign-in, signed-in devices and suspending sit on each person's page. Two-step sign-in is required for the owner and anyone who approves or pays money.
        11. Every outside connection, and whether it works, on one Integrations page.
        12. On a phone, the sidebar becomes a top bar and a full-screen menu.
        13. The model portal is not part of this and does not change.
        14. Production, Finance and People appear to show where they fit. Their rows on Today are examples, not designs; each gets its own mockup when its turn comes.

        Considered and not chosen: a narrow strip of department icons with a second column for sections. It takes about 64 pixels that the Inbox's three columns need, and icons without names make people guess.

        For the engineers: how the frame would be built
        • Each department declares itself once: its name, icon, what a person must hold, its sections, what it adds to Today and what it adds to search. The sidebar, Today, search and the address rules are built from that list, so adding a department touches one file instead of the role switchboard in App.tsx.
        • A person's departments are the union of all their live roles (several roles per person). The server still checks every request; the sidebar only hides what the server already refuses.
        • The shell is one component made of parts (Shell.Sidebar, Shell.Head, Shell.Main) instead of today's SideShell with flags such as flush and immersive; a full-width screen like Chats uses its own part rather than a flag.
        • Old addresses (/payments, /payroll, /targets, /operations/…) redirect to the department's address, so bookmarks and links in emails keep working.
        • The order page reads the existing tables by Shopify's order id. No table is merged.
