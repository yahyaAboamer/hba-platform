Production inside the HBA frame

How HBA's frame sits around Production without changing it. While Production is open, HBA's sidebar folds into a narrow strip, and everything to its right is Production exactly as it is today. Production's screens here are redrawn from the live app's own colours, type and sizes, with made-up names and numbers; the real screens come from its source code, unchanged. Nothing here is built yet.

1. Production on a laptop

HBA's sidebar folds to a strip 64 pixels wide. The other 1216 are Production as it is today, which at that width shows its cards three across, as it does now. Every department keeps its icon, its count and its red failure dot, and pointing at an icon names it, as here on the Inbox. The button under the H opens the names (frame 2); try it.

Inbox · 6 chats waiting for a reply

2. Department names, when asked for

The button under the H opens HBA's full list over Production, without moving it: the same departments, counts, search and Settings as the full sidebar everywhere else. Choosing a department, clicking outside or pressing Esc closes it. Production underneath stays exactly where it was.

3. In and out, in the light theme

Someone who chose the light theme sees every other department light, with the full sidebar. Opening Production folds the sidebar into a strip that is dark, because Production has only a dark look and a white strip beside it would glare. Leaving Production unfolds the sidebar again, in the person's own theme.

What you are approving

  1. While Production is open, HBA's sidebar folds to a strip 64 pixels wide. Everything to its right is Production exactly as it is today. Every other department keeps the full sidebar, as approved.
  2. The strip holds the H (which opens Today), a button that shows the department names, search, the departments the person holds, then Settings and the person at the bottom. Pointing at an icon names it; counts and the red failure dot stay.
  3. The names button opens HBA's full list over Production without moving it. Choosing a department, clicking outside or pressing Esc closes it.
  4. Production keeps its own sidebar, top bar, search, alerts and person menu. HBA's search looks across every department; Production's looks inside Production.
  5. The strip is dark while Production is open, whatever theme the person chose, because Production has only a dark look. Every other department follows the person's theme.
  6. Production is marked in the strip in grey, not HBA green, so HBA's green never sits beside Production's lime.
  7. On a phone, a thin HBA bar with a Departments button sits above Production's own bar.
  8. Suppliers see only their supplier portal, full screen, with no HBA frame at all, as models see only their portal.
  9. Production's person menu shows the HBA account, and its "Sign in to edit" box no longer appears, because everyone arrives signed in. What it shows changes; how it looks does not.

Considered and not chosen: HBA's full sidebar beside Production's own. Two sidebars take 468 of a laptop's 1280 pixels and push Production's cards from three across to two, which would change how it looks.

Also not chosen: Production full screen with only a floating HBA button. It hides which department you are in and every count waiting elsewhere, and a floating button covers part of Production's own screen.
