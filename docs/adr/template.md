# NNNN. Title stating the decision

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What was true that forced a choice. Include the constraint or the failure that
prompted it, and any evidence gathered. Someone reading this in three years has
none of the surrounding conversation.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Consequences

What this costs. Include what it makes harder, not only what it makes possible -
an ADR that lists no downside is usually hiding one.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it was not chosen. This is often the most
useful section: it stops the same rejected idea being re-proposed every year.
