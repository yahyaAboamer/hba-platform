# Decision — D04

**Owner's question:** Can an incoming deduction take a later
guarantee-qualified month below its guaranteed minimum, or only consume
earnings above the floor?

**Put to him as the act rather than the rule**, 10 September 2026: a model was
overpaid for August because an order failed after the month was agreed. She is
on a guaranteed minimum of E£8,000. October's commission comes to E£9,000. How
much of the August overpayment can October absorb?

**Owner's actual answer:** *the whole E£9,000 if needed.*

**Status:** confirmed.

---

## What it settles

**Recovery comes before the guarantee.** An overpayment carried into a later
month consumes that month's whole payable, not only the part earned above the
guaranteed minimum. A month can settle at zero while the debt clears.

This applies to a month the model **qualified** for her guarantee in. The
guarantee decides what the month is *worth*; it does not protect what is
*paid out* of it against money already advanced.

## What it does not settle

**It is not automatic absorption.** §11.5's choice is untouched: HBA still
decides between carrying an overpayment into a later month (a credit) and
absorbing it (a write-off). D04 answers only *how much a month can take* once
carrying is chosen.

**It does not change what a month is worth.** The guarantee still applies to
the calculation exactly as §15 says, and the snapshot still freezes it. What
changes is how much of the resulting payable a deduction may consume.

**It says nothing about D09** — a failure that later turns out to be wrong,
after a deduction has been used. That is still open.

## The recommendation this overrules, and why it is recorded

The platform's recommendation was the other option: protect the floor, take
only what was earned above it, and let the remainder wait for a later month.
The reasoning was that a guaranteed minimum is a promise about a month, and a
month that pays nothing is not obviously distinguishable — from the model's
side — from a month where the promise was broken.

The owner chose recovery first, and it is his money and his relationship with
the people involved. Recorded here because a later reader finding a model paid
E£0 in a month she met her targets in should find the decision rather than a
bug report.

**What it obliges the interface to do.** Because the reasoning is invisible
from her side, the screens carry it: a month reduced by a deduction says so, in
her own view, with what was advanced and what is left. §11.5 already requires
an adjustment to be visible to the person it was made about — *a credit she
cannot see is a credit she cannot check* — and this decision is why that line
matters more than it did.
