Review states
November 2026 — ordinary open month. Delivered and pending both counted, failed excluded.
October 2026 — approved, payment not yet recorded, and carries the September recovery as a separate line.
September 2026 — paid, then an order failed. Approved figure and recorded payment stay fixed; the month's sales performance now differs, explained in How this adds up.
February and January 2026 — settled with no imported receipt. Performance history intact.
August 2026 — content targets missed. Commission and salary are unaffected; only the guarantee does not apply.
Changed in this revision
Pending orders now count towards sales and commission; no “on delivery” wording, and nothing counts twice.
Sales chart shows amounts, Uses chart shows counts with the count in the tooltip and above each bar.
Order breakdown accounts for every order in the month, failed included.
Percentage comparisons replaced with a stated two-period comparison in EGP.
Three compensation arrangements as review examples, switched outside the app.
Ranking uses the supplied counts — no estimate from average order value — and explains the ordering.
Promotion requests are display-only: no posted state, counts or completion styling.
Payment details ask for what each method needs: InstaPay account number and payment link (with a help image behind ⓘ), wallet provider and number, or account holder, card number and bank.
Explanations that used to sit as body text — the later deduction and the guaranteed minimum — now open from an ⓘ beside their own row.
Theme lives in You. History starts January 2026 and the picker and chart both start there.
Body text 14px, secondary 12.5–13px, touch targets 46px and up.
